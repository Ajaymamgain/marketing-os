Start with the operational decision

Over the past few weeks, I’ve been using Jev from TypeSafe to experiment with bounded decisions for MarkOps: interpreting an account’s current state and selecting the next step within a workflow we control.

Marketing operations rarely struggles with a shortage of signals. It struggles with turning those signals into the right action, in the right system, with an explanation somebody can trust.

A form submission sits in one platform. Program membership appears in another export. An account description is incomplete. Advertising data arrives as campaign totals. The team ends up reconciling spreadsheets, maintaining lists and asking the same question: what should happen next?

That is the problem we are building Dual Motion to address. The organizing unit is an account and a specific need. A company can have several needs, each with its own evidence, score and next action. Every contact at a company should not be treated as if they were pursuing the same outcome.

Activity rules and segment definitions are configurable. A high composite does not independently grant permission to act. Coverage, consent, destination mapping and activation holds still matter.

Enrichment should close an evidence gap

When company context is missing, research can help. We use Parallel AI to find official company sources and extract supporting passages for business descriptions, products, industry and public professional roles.

The value is the evidence chain: a source URL, a supporting passage and the time it was observed. A similarly named company should not become a match simply because it appears in a search result.

Parallel AI also supports data connections. Our current integration uses search and extraction; additional connections need their own configuration, access and validation. Research findings remain candidates for review.

A connection is only the beginning

A successful connection tells us that tools are available. It does not tell us that useful data has been imported or that a campaign action succeeded.

Marketo is our first implemented native marketing connector. In the latest local verification, a read-only catalog sync brought 253 real source assets into Dual Motion.

A smart list remains a dynamic query evaluated by Marketo. A static list remains explicit membership in Marketo. Importing their names is different from reading their members, resolving them to accounts or enrolling somebody in a campaign.

Asset topics help organize programs, campaigns and lists. Account segments describe supported business context or observed interests. Neither is permission to enroll a person.

Bring history forward without inventing fresh intent

A new customer should not need to start from an empty event ledger. Historical imports validate identities, account domains, campaign-to-need mappings and timestamps before accepted rows are committed.

Original event dates are preserved. Duplicate events are excluded. Unresolved records are quarantined with reasons. Accepted activity can enter the scoring and recommendation queue, while delivery stays on hold until the migration is reviewed.

Old activity should become useful history, not a new buying signal.

Advertising exports are useful for campaign reporting. Campaign-level clicks and conversions do not identify an individual buyer, so they stay separate from person-level intent.

Chart replay has a different purpose: inspect historical data and demonstrate the timeline. Pressing Play changes the displayed chart; it does not modify scores or execute marketing actions.

Make the next action accountable

The workflow is straightforward: a recorded event informs the current score, a recommendation proposes the next step, eligibility checks decide whether it can proceed, and a mapped playbook records the delivery outcome.

Existing playbook controls govern Marketo list, program and eligible campaign actions. Customer destinations must be mapped and verified before use. The local environment remains in dry run. Broader Google Ads, Meta Ads and LinkedIn Ads activation is planned.

Operational support should help a team review an import, investigate failed delivery, research missing company evidence or prepare a scoring configuration improvement. These are bounded responsibilities with recorded evidence and reviewable changes.

Automation needs a defined source, an identified subject, a relevant need, an eligible destination and an outcome somebody can inspect.

Give the team a clear operating control

Automatic decisions has a simple On/Off setting with Save. Off pauses new automatic recommendations, enrichment and segment classification. Data sync, imports and recorded scores remain available.

The point is to let teams operate deliberately: inspect data, test a migration and review destinations before activating automatic decision workflows.
